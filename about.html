About Off Block

What is Off Block?

Off Block is a flight delay prediction tool built as an IBDP Computer Science Internal Assessment project. It helps passengers check the delay probability of upcoming flights departing from Istanbul Airport (IST).

How does it work?

The system analyzes historical flight data, weather conditions, time of day, and route information to estimate the likelihood of a delay for any given flight.
